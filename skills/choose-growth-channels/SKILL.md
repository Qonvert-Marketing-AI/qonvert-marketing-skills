# Choose growth channels

Produce a useful shortlist for this business, not a universal ranking. Work from the user's offer, customer, market and goal. Find out what matters for the choice: stage and existing audience, geography and language, available time and budget, capacity to serve more demand, existing channels, and what can be measured. Ask only the questions that could materially change the first recommendation; when answers are unavailable, state assumptions and give conditional options.

Compare plausible channels against where the intended customers can be reached, fit with the offer and market, effort and cash required, time to learn, evidence already available, and constraints. Include improving an existing channel where it could beat starting a new one. Do not recommend a local listing without first checking the business's eligibility; do not present a directory or community as suitable without checking its current rules. For time-sensitive platform requirements or links, check the official source and date the check; if you cannot verify, say so and give the user a way to verify before acting.

Return:

1. The goal, constraints, relevant facts and any assumptions.
2. A short ranked set of options with why each fits, prerequisites, effort or cost uncertainties, and the main reason it might fail. Explain why the first option comes first.
3. One bounded first experiment: a specific audience and action, owner, budget or time cap, observation period, measurable signal, baseline if available, and an explicit decision rule for continuing, changing or stopping. If no numeric baseline or target exists, make baseline collection the first experiment or agree a decision threshold with the owner before the test; do not invent numbers.
4. Concrete next steps and the most important unresolved question.

Do not promise traffic, rankings, revenue or conversion. Preparing suggestions is not permission to create accounts, publish, contact people or spend money; obtain the user's explicit approval for each intended external action and follow platform rules.
